# Niche

**Chosen:** AI tools & productivity software reviews/roundups.

**Why:**
- Matches what the agents on this machine already research and write well.
- SaaS affiliate programs commonly pay 20–40% **recurring** commission (e.g., Jasper, Writesonic, Descript via PartnerStack/Impact) — recurring beats one-off Amazon payouts for a €200/mo target.
- Buyer-intent keywords ("X review", "best X tools") convert without needing huge traffic: rough math — €200/mo ≈ 60 conversions at €3.50 avg first-month commission, ≈ 4–6k targeted visits/mo.

**Affiliate economics baked into topic selection:**
1. Roundups ("best X for Y") — rank easier, internal-link to reviews.
2. Reviews ("X review") — highest buyer intent, money pages.
