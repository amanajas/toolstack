Subject: Request for authorisation — outside interest (§11.2 of employment contract)

for a new outside interest I want to embark on.

- Personal writing/activity guidance: sponsor/topic does not conflict with Alphabet;

Purpose of this note: to obtain the prior authorisation required by §11.2. I'm happy to add
any detail you need, align on any conditions, or walk through quickly in person/via call.
