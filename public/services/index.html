End-to-End Projects, Done Privately

You bring a task and the files it needs. I take it from start to finish and hand back the finished result. All the work happens on my own private AI setup — so your files never pass through any cloud AI service, and nothing about the job leaves my infrastructure.

The privacy rule

Your files stay private. Always. No cloud AI accounts, no API keys, no subscriptions — there is simply no path from your material to the internet. The assistant that processes your work runs on my own hardware, behind my own walls.

What a project looks like

  1. You describe the task and hand over the material (documents, data, references).
  2. I do the work end to end — processing, drafting, structuring, checking — on my private setup.
  3. You receive the finished result in the format you need, on time, with a short note on how it was produced.

What I don’t do

  • No consulting — I don’t advise on AI strategy or review your plans.
  • No coaching — no courses, no live training sessions.
