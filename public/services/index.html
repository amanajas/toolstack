Private AI Assistant — Your Files Stay Private

1 January 0001

A private AI assistant is like having a capable colleague who has read all your documents — and who cannot leak them. It is proven, openly available AI software that I install and configure on hardware you control. It answers questions about your files, drafts and summarizes your work, and never sends a single byte of your client data to a third-party service.

The rule we set up around

Your client files stay private. Always. Documents are stored and processed on your own server or a dedicated machine in your office. No cloud AI provider ever sees them. We set it up so this is enforced by architecture, not by policy — there is simply no path from your files to the internet.

What the assistant does

  • Answers questions about your documents — contracts, cases, patient or client records, policies — reading your whole document corpus, not fragments
  • Works with your existing tools — your CRM, Google Workspace, document store — without the data leaving your network
  • Serves your whole team through a simple web interface, with per-role access
  • Uses a capable, current AI model — modern assistant capability, on your hardware

What I don’t do

  • No consulting — I don’t advise on your AI strategy or review your plans. I install and deliver working systems; I don’t sell opinions.
  • No coaching — I don’t teach courses or train your staff beyond the handoff session for the system I installed.

If you need advice rather than a working system, I’m not the right hire — and I’ll say so instead of selling you the wrong thing.

How engagements work

Fixed price, clear deliverables, no open-ended consulting. Every project ends with a working assistant, documentation, and a handoff session for your team. If we cannot show you the result on a screen-share before you commit, we don’t deserve the job.
