Private AI Assistant — Your Files Stay Private

1 January 0001

A private AI assistant is like having a capable colleague who has read all your documents — and who cannot leak them. It is proven, openly available AI software that I prepare for you and hand over as a finished, ready-to-use product.

How it works

  1. You come to me and describe what the assistant should do and which documents it should know.
  2. I prepare everything on my own hardware. The assistant is built, tested, and locked down before you ever receive it. Your files are handled on my equipment under the same privacy rule — nothing goes to any cloud service.
  3. You receive a finished product: the assistant on its own machine (or as a package ready to run on yours), with documentation and a recorded walkthrough instead of live training.

The privacy rule

Your client files stay private. Always. Nothing is sent to any cloud AI provider — there is no account, no API key, no subscription to any external AI service. The assistant works from its own storage, offline if you want.

What the assistant can do

  • Answer questions about a defined set of documents — contracts, cases, records, policies — reading the whole corpus, not fragments
  • Draft and summarize based on those documents
  • Run without any internet connection, so nothing can leave the machine

What I don’t do

  • No consulting — I don’t advise on your AI strategy or review your plans.
  • No coaching — you get documentation and a recorded walkthrough, not live training sessions.
  • No working inside your systems — I never need access to your network, your CRM, or your accounts. I prepare the product; you own and operate it.
