AI writing tools have split into clear camps: marketing copy platforms, long-form assistants, and grammar/polish layers. This guide compares the strongest options and links to full reviews. **Pricing changes often — always check current pricing on the vendor's site.**

## Quick comparison

| Tool | Best for | Free tier | Standout feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jasper | Marketing teams | Trial only | Brand voice + campaigns |
| Copy.ai | Sales/marketing workflows | Limited free | Workflow automation (GTM) |
| Writesonic | SEO content at scale | Limited free | Real-time SEO checks |
| Rytr | Budget users | Generous free | Cheap unlimited plan |
| Anyword | Ad copy | Trial | Predictive performance scores |
| Wordtune | Rewriting | Limited free | Sentence-level rewrites |
| Grammarly | Polish everywhere | Yes | Cross-app integration |
| Notion AI | Notes + docs users | Paid add-on | Writing inside your workspace |
| ChatGPT | General-purpose | Yes | Flexibility |

## How to choose

1. **You run marketing content at scale** — a platform with SEO integrations and team features (Jasper, Writesonic, Surfer-style optimization) pays for itself.
2. **You write ads or short conversion copy** — Anyword's predictive scoring is unique; Copy.ai's workflow automation handles repetitive outbound.
3. **You mostly draft once and edit a lot** — Wordtune or Grammarly beat a full generation platform.
4. **Budget is the constraint** — Rytr offers the lowest cost per month with an actually usable free tier.
5. **You already live in Notion** — Notion AI removes the app-switching cost entirely.

## What actually differs between them

Most tools use the same family of underlying models. The difference is the work layer: prompt pipelines, tone memory, plagiarism checks, SEO data feeds, and integrations. That matters more than the raw model — a tool wired into your search-data workflow will outperform a raw chatbot for production content.

## Honesty notes

- AI-drafted content performs when it has *human editing and real expertise* layered in. Google's guidance targets unhelpful mass-produced content, not AI assistance per se.
- Annual plans are much cheaper than monthly; trial lengths change frequently.
- "Unlimited words" plans usually throttle quality settings before volume.

## Alternatives

If none of these fit, general assistants like ChatGPT or Claude with a good prompt template cover most single-person use cases — you trade convenience features for flexibility and a lower bill.
